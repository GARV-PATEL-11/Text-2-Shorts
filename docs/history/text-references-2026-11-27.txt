FARADAY — REFERENCE WORDINGS FOR EVERY USER-FACING TEXT (2026-11-27)
====================================================================

A proposal only: no text in the code was changed. It goes row by row through the text inventory in
docs/history/text-audit-2026-11-27.md (rows A1–A229, B1–B259, C1–C300). For each text it gives a verdict
and, where a reference fits, the candidate lines from games, films, series, great people, books, GitHub,
Stack Overflow and developer folklore, each with the page it was checked against.


HOW SHUFFLING ALREADY WORKS (nothing new to build)
--------------------------------------------------
- frontend/src/config/voice.ts holds the catalogue: LINES (text, source, url) and VOICE (each "moment" lists
  the lines it may show). There are 25 moments today: welcome, homeLead, nothingInProgress, noRuns,
  chooseLook, question, audience, review, readyToBegin, openingRun, loading, processing, pending,
  rendering, asks, nothingToDecide, approved, complete, noChanges, failed, renderFailed, offline,
  restricted, lost and closing.
- frontend/src/components/ui/Said.tsx, useLine(moment): picks ONE line at random, once per visit
  (useState initialiser), so the page never flickers on re-render. This is the random shuffle you asked for.
- Adding alternatives = adding a line to LINES and its key to a moment's array. A new moment = one new key in
  VOICE plus <Said moment="…" /> where the text is shown. No new mechanism is needed.
- Plain-text VARIANTS (equal-meaning titles such as loading messages) can reuse the same mechanism: a Line
  with no `source` renders as plain text (see splash() in voice.ts).


RULES EVERY ENTRY BELOW FOLLOWS
-------------------------------
1. The instruction is never shuffled or replaced. A quote goes BESIDE the plain text ("Could Not Load the
   Script" + a line), never instead of it. The plain text is what the user acts on, what the tests match,
   what a screen reader announces and what someone quotes when they ask for help.
2. Labels, buttons, field names, aria text, approvals, change requests, sign-in and anything destructive stay
   plain (DESIGN_REFERENCE.md "Tone by moment"). Verdict KEEP PLAIN; a clearer plain rewording is given
   where the audit found a problem.
3. Machine-read text never gets references or shuffling: driver/ask_user messages, hook output, checkpoint
   problems Claude acts on, CLI lines that tests or scripts parse, API error bodies.
4. Failure moments stay sober. A joke on the fifth failed render grates; the lines proposed for failure are
   calm or encouraging, not funny.
5. Verified or not proposed. Each line was fetched word for word on the URL given (Wikiquote sourced
   sections, Wikisource, the RFC or the product's own page). Misattributed or disputed lines are listed
   at the end as rejected, together with DESIGN_REFERENCE.md's existing rejected list.
6. Copyright: short, credited quotations (≤ 20 words); Faraday's words are public domain. NO song lyrics.
   → The current catalogue breaks this once: "This was a triumph." (moment `complete`) is the first line
     of the song "Still Alive" from Portal. Recommend removing it.
7. No brand or character names inside the plain UI text; the credit line under a quote is the only place
   a film, game or book is named.


VERDICTS USED
-------------
KEEP PLAIN               label/button/aria/approval/exact error; optional clearer plain rewording
PLAIN REWORD             developer text or a raw error shown to users; the plain wording to use instead
PLAIN + QUOTE (moment)   the text stays; the moment shows one of the listed lines, shuffled per visit
VARIANTS                 equal-meaning plain wordings that may be shuffled (titles of waits, empty states)

RECONCILED DECISIONS (read this before the three parts; it overrides them where they disagree)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three researchers wrote the parts independently. Where they disagree, this section wins.

Remove from the current catalogue (voice.ts):
- triumph    "This was a triumph."  Song lyric (Portal, "Still Alive"). Part 2 (B180) and Part 3 (cliDone) still
             list it as an option; ignore those mentions.
- failure    "Failure is not an option!"  On a failure screen it reads as blame.
- badFeeling "I have a very bad feeling about this."  Adds dread to a failure.
- tears      "…like tears in rain."  About dying; the moment `lost` is just a missing run. Use github404/droids.
- impossible (Portal) in renderFailed: tells the user the task is impossible when a retry usually works.
- wizard (Gandalf) out of `pending` and `rendering`: says slowness is intended. It may stay in `usageHigh`
             (Part 3), where waiting really is the advice.
Part 2 keeps failure/badFeeling in `failed`; Part 1 removes them. Removing wins (rule 4, failures stay sober).

                guessing, didntGive

  fExamine, f_noPoet = fPoem, f_setToWork = fProper, picardMakeIt = makeItSo = picard, f_workFinish = fWork,

NOTES (risks)
=====================================================================================================
1. Machine-read text is the main risk. KEEP PLAIN rows were chosen because tests, hooks, Claude or UNFORCEABLE substring
   matching read them: C2, C12, C17-C19, C38-C39, C49-C52, C61, C64-C68, C69-C139 (as Claude-facing), C143-C147,
   C210-C274, C275-C300. Rewording any of these for the site must go through a separate user_text field, not by editing the
   string Claude receives.
2. C64/C68/C124: "final.mp4 is missing or out of date", "is not built from its current inputs", "preview missing or out of
   date" are matched as substrings to decide what "--force" may not override (approvals.py:112). Do not reword them in place.
3. CLI human lines must never break parsed output: print the quote on its own line AFTER the machine line, with a blank
   line before it, and keep it off stderr and off any command that supports --json or is read by Claude (approve, validate,
   hash, status, next, report, template, feedback, resolve, usage-ok). Safe commands: serve, new, final, --help, templates.
4. No lyrics are proposed. All quote lines are at most 24 words (k:fPoem 23, the longest). Fragments of public-domain text are
   shown word for word from Wikisource or Wikiquote; k:lovelaceLoom is a fragment with a leading ellipsis.
5. Wikiquote verification went through WebFetch's summariser, which returned the exact line and its section; the Faraday
   Candle lines were also matched word for word with grep on the Wikisource text (lectures I to IV fetched; V and VI were
   rate-limited and not used).
6. Wikiquote's Star Wars page does not mark the Dialogue section as sourced; the existing voice.ts lines from that page are
   in the same state. If a stricter rule is wanted, drop k:starWars.
7. Tone: 'houston' is mild but appears in both failed and renderFailed; the new notFound and serverError screens would add a
   third use. If it feels repetitive, serverError should use k:fTry and notFound should use k:starWars alone.
8. 'Make it so.' (k:picard) is a catchphrase, not a long quote, and carries a brand (Star Trek) only in the credit line.
9. Look & Sound history clauses are facts, not quotes. They must go in a second line under the plain explanation and never
   replace it. The font and typeface claims were each read on the Wikipedia page named; no dates beyond those pages are added.
10. Spelling: the house style in labels is British (Colour, Centre) but the audit also shows Math/Color in some labels
    (C148). Pick one before rewording C148-C152.
11. Rows C34, C35, C36, C204 and the internal log section need no change.
12. Row count: all of C1-C300 are covered above; groups like C69-C139 and C210-C274 are compressed by shared verdict,
    with individual rewordings given where the audit flagged developer text.
