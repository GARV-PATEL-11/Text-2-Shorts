# Reviewing the script before CP1

Check the whole `script.json` against the brief, fix what fails, then run the accuracy-reviewer (script mode).
Report to the user only what you changed and anything you could not resolve.

1. Objectives and scope: every brief objective is covered by a scene that actually teaches it; nothing important
   for the audience is missing; nothing outside the brief's scope or constraints.
2. Accuracy: concepts, facts and definitions are correct; no simplification that leaves a wrong mental model;
   terms are used precisely and consistently (the accuracy-reviewer checks this independently too).
3. Progression: prerequisites come first; each scene has one distinct objective; no two scenes repeat each other;
   the last scene closes the loop with the objectives.
4. Granularity: no scene carries more ideas than its duration allows; complex ideas are spread across scenes.
5. Narration: reads naturally aloud; follows the narration convention and the explanation style (standards.md);
   every new term explained where it first appears; concrete before abstract.
6. Bridges: every scene after the first opens with a bridge that says what was covered, what comes next and why
   it matters (transitions.md); no bridge repeats the previous scene's narration word for word.
7. Timing and pacing: word counts fit each scene's duration; durations fit the requested length; harder scenes get
   more time; pauses after key results.
